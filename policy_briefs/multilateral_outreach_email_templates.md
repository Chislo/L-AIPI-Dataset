I am writing to share a newly released macroeconomic panel dataset that may be of interest to your research group: the **Longitudinal AI Preparedness Index (L-AIPI)**, covering 149 developing economies (and 240+ global territories) annually from 2000 to 2023.

Until now, empirical cross-country studies on AI have been limited by the lack of historical time series. Building on the multi-pillar framework of the IMF AI Preparedness Index (Cazzaniga et al., 2024), we reconstructed continuous time-varying readiness measures across digital infrastructure, human capital, and innovation capacity ($r = 0.912$ against the official 2023 IMF benchmark).

In our paper, we deploy this panel to estimate the moderating effect of AI readiness on climate shocks in agriculture using two-way fixed effects and dynamic local projections (Jordà, 2005), formalizing an *AI Adaptation Capacity Paradox*.

Because L-AIPI is formatted as a standardized, ready-to-merge panel (`iso3`, `year`, Stata `.dta` and CSV), it can be readily integrated into research examining technology diffusion, labor reallocation, structural transformation, FDI, and trade.
